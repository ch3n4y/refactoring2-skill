# Source map

The skill was distilled from the Chinese text of Martin Fowler's *Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code, Second Edition*. The skill paraphrases concepts and adds an agent-oriented workflow; it does not copy the book's examples.

- [Upstream source repository](https://github.com/NxeedGoto/Refactoring2-zh)
